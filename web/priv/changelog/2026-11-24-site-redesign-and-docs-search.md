---
title: A new look for the site, and documentation search
date: 2026-11-24
---

The Struo site has a new dark design, shared by the home page, the blog, the
changelog, and the documentation.

The documentation now has search. Press `Ctrl K`, or `⌘ K` on a Mac, or use the
search field at the top of the sidebar to find pages, sections, and text.
